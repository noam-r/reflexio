# Preparatory Adjustments

This page defines the forward-looking preparation operations that Reflexio modules can expose before a future event occurs.

## Purpose

Introduce the prospective counterpart to reflective adjustment: bounded preparation operations that improve framing before an event rather than reinterpretation after it.

## Core Idea

A preparatory adjustment is a bounded, forward-looking revision to expectation, framing, wording, timing, scope, success criteria, or self-protective structure that helps a person enter a future event in a healthier and more adaptive way.

Reflective adjustments help reinterpret the past. Preparatory adjustments help shape the future.

## Why Preparatory Adjustments Matter

In many difficult situations, the most important intervention is often not selecting one branch mechanically. It is improving how the future event is framed before action occurs.

A user may need help to:
- reduce over-rigid expectations
- separate hope from certainty
- set a better goal for the conversation
- define what counts as success
- prepare for discomfort without catastrophizing it
- choose wording that lowers the chance of destructive appraisal
- preserve self-respect even if the outcome is imperfect

These are all preparatory adjustments.

## Common Adjustment Targets

Preparatory adjustments may focus on:
- expectation calibration
- success criteria
- wording and tone
- timing
- boundary clarity
- ambiguity tolerance
- emotional preparedness
- separation of self-worth from one outcome
- choice of action path

These are the main levers through which healthier prospective processing can happen.

## Example

Suppose a user wants to ask for a raise.

A poor internal framing might be:
- “If they say no, it means I am undervalued and should feel ashamed.”

A healthier preparatory adjustment might be:
- “The goal of this conversation is to get information, make a clear case, and preserve self-respect, even if the answer is not immediately yes.”

This does not guarantee success, but it creates a more adaptive entry into the event.

## Preparatory vs Reflective Adjustment

### Reflective adjustment
Revises the processing chain of a past event.

### Preparatory adjustment
Improves the framing, expectation, or action plan for a future event.

Both are built on the same architecture, but they point in different temporal directions.

## Why This Fits Reflexio

Preparatory adjustments reuse the same core principles:
- expectation matters
- discrepancy matters
- appraisal matters
- healthier processing is more proportional and less distorted

The difference is that the intervention happens before the event rather than after it.

## Design Rule

A preparatory adjustment should improve readiness and framing without pretending to control the future or collapse projected outcomes into certainty.

## How It Connects

These pages define the initial forward-looking extension of Reflexio. In a later iteration, this module can be linked directly into the main processing flow and visualized as a parallel branch to retrospective reflection.
